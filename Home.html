Problem Statement

Teachers generally observe students’ expressions to determine whether they are sufficiently attentive, this method consumes a significant amount of the teacher’s energy. Furthermore, it is difficult to determine whether students are attentive or not.

Proposed Solution

It is essential to measure the student’s concentration level in class. This will help the instructor to assess the student and obtain valuable feedback as to whether the learning goals have been achieved and to identify the type of intervention needed to improve learning outcomes. Therefore, there is a need to create a model that shows the level of understanding of students in a class.

Methodology

Developing a model to detect the student’s mental state, whether he/she is concentrating or in a relaxed mode. This model consists of different stages such as EEG signal acquisition, preprocessing, feature extraction, and classification. The machine learning algorithms that will be used in mental state classification are Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting Regression, and Voting Regression.
